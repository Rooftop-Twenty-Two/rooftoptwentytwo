faq:
  - question: "Which platform should we go with?"
    answer: "It depends on what the site has to do. Selling products? Shopify, nine times out of ten. Lots of content and a team that knows WordPress? WordPress. A service business that needs enquiries and a fast site? Custom code. We'll give you a straight recommendation on the first call."
  - question: "We're already on WordPress. Do we have to move?"
    answer: "No. If it's working and your team is happy in it, we'll redesign it and tidy up what's underneath. We only suggest moving when the platform itself is holding you back."
  - question: "Can our team still edit a custom-coded site?"
    answer: "Yes. You get a visual editor for the things that change often, like blog posts, case studies and team members. Nobody needs to touch code. New page layouts come through us, and they're quick."
  - question: "What's custom code actually built with?"
    answer: "Mostly Astro, with a visual editor on top for your team. Next.js when the site behaves more like an app, with logins or live data. It's hosted on modern platforms like Vercel. You don't need to know any of that to use it, but it's there if your IT people ask."
  - question: "What does the AI actually do?"
    answer: "It does the legwork inside the code. Drafting a new section, fixing a layout, checking for broken links. A developer reviews every change before it goes live. It's why small changes come back in hours, not a week."
  - question: "Do you work on Shopify stores we already have?"
    answer: "Yes. We redesign stores, build out new sections, sort speed issues and connect the apps you rely on. You don't always need a full rebuild to get a better shop."
  - question: "Will we lose our Google rankings if we move platform?"
    answer: "Not if it's done properly. We map every old URL to its new home with a redirect, carry over page titles and descriptions, and keep a close eye on Search Console after launch."
  - question: "Do you build in Webflow or Framer?"
    answer: "No. We build in WordPress, Shopify and custom code, and we're good at those. If you're on Webflow or Framer and want to move, we can bring you across."
  - question: "How long does a website project take?"
    answer: "Most websites take 4 to 10 weeks, depending on the size of the build. A focused marketing site lands at the shorter end. Bigger sites with custom integrations or a large shop take longer. We'll give you a clear timeline once we've scoped it together."
  - question: "How do you price it?"
    answer: "Every site is different, so we quote against a written scope rather than a price list. Integrations, shop setup and content migration are all factored in. Tell us what you need and we'll come back with a clear proposal."
  - question: "What happens after launch?"
    answer: "Our care plan kicks in. Hosting, security updates, content changes and a monthly look at how the site is performing, with fixes where the numbers say we need them."

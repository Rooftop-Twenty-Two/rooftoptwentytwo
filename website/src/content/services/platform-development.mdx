faq:
  - question: "How do we know if we need a platform?"
    answer: "If people need to log in, book, apply, upload, track or manage something, you probably need a platform. If they mostly need to find out about you and get in touch, a website will do. Plenty of businesses need both, and we build both."
  - question: "How long does a platform take to build?"
    answer: "It depends on the scope. A focused first version can go live in a couple of months. Bigger platforms are usually built in phases, so you get something working early and add to it. We'll give you a clear timeline once we've scoped it together."
  - question: "How do you price it?"
    answer: "Against a written scope and functional specification. Every feature is agreed upfront, so you know what you're getting, and any changes later are priced against that same baseline. No surprises."
  - question: "Can it connect to our existing systems?"
    answer: "Usually, yes. We've connected platforms to CRMs like ActiveCampaign, email and SMS tools, video hosting, booking tools and more. We'll check your systems during discovery."
  - question: "What about security and data protection?"
    answer: "We build with roles and permissions from the start, so people only see what they should. Data is hosted in the EU where possible, and for regulated businesses we design around record-keeping requirements like versioned terms and audit trails."
  - question: "Who owns the platform?"
    answer: "You do. The code, the data and the accounts are yours. We're happy to keep supporting and developing it, but you're never locked in."

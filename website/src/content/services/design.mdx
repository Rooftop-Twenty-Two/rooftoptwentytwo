faq:
  - question: "Do you do brand refreshes as well as full rebrands?"
    answer: "Yes. Refreshes preserve recognition (typically the mark and the core palette) and modernise the system around them. Full rebrands rebuild the visual identity from positioning up. We'll recommend the right one after the discovery audit."
  - question: "How long does an identity project take?"
    answer: "An identity on its own usually takes 6–10 weeks from strategy session to final guidelines. With the website, packaging or print included, allow 12–20 weeks depending on scope."
  - question: "What do we get at the end?"
    answer: "Your brand strategy, the final logo in every format you need, brand guidelines, source files and a launch toolkit for signage, social and stationery. And the same team on hand while you roll it out."
  - question: "Can you work with our existing brand?"
    answer: "Yes. A lot of our design work is campaign and applied design on existing systems. We respect the brand we inherit and flag where we'd recommend evolving it."

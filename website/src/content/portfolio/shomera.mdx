  - number: "03"
    heading: "Discovery and strategy"
    body: "We started by listening. A kick-off in February, then a brand discovery workshop with David O'Reilly and the Shomera team, where we got into who they really want to work with and why people pick them. Alongside that we audited every touchpoint they had, from the website to the roadside signs, and reviewed ten competitors across garden rooms, extensions and retrofit. The category was a sea of green, grey and timber. Safe fonts, template websites, engineering diagrams. Almost nobody was using design with real discipline. That was the gap. The strategy landed on one idea: built with certainty. Building work is stressful because it's uncertain. Dates slip, costs creep, and nobody owns the problem. Shomera designs, manages and builds everything itself, so one team answers for the lot. Certainty became the thing to sell."

  - number: "04"
    heading: "Brand identity"
    body: "Two concepts went on the table. One built on authority and discipline, one on process and coordination. Shomera wanted the confidence of the first, with more warmth and heritage, so we went again until it was right. The final identity keeps what people already knew. Shomera's red stays, deepened into a heritage red that feels established rather than loud. It sits with midnight slate and a set of warm neutrals (sage, stone, sandstone and taupe) so the brand reads calm and architectural, and nothing like the category's greens. The wordmark is the one people recognise, redrawn heavier, with a single line running over it and down one side. A frame. Structure, said simply. GT Standard carries the type: medium for headlines, light for reading. Then we rolled it out everywhere a customer meets Shomera:"

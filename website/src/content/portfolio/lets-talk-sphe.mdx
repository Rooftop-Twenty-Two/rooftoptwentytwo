    body: "SPHE is changing in Irish schools. From September 2027, every school has to deliver 60 hours of SPHE at senior cycle, plus an hour a week in Transition Year. Richie Sadlier, former Ireland international, broadcaster and one of the country's best-known voices on young people's wellbeing, set up Let's Talk SPHE with Pam O'Leary so teachers wouldn't have to build those lessons from scratch. Richie is the heart of it. Teachers and students trust him, and the platform had to carry that same voice. We built the whole thing: the teacher platform, the public website, and the tools schools use to get their teachers set up."

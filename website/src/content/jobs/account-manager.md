intro: "We're a growing digital marketing agency in Glasnevin, working with a broad range of Irish businesses across paid media, SEO, social, email, design, video and web. There are fourteen of us. We work closely together, know our clients well and take pride in getting good work done. We move quickly and give people real responsibility. In this role you'll own a portfolio of clients and lead their day-to-day marketing. You'll learn each client's business, lead conversations with confidence and know how the marketing supports their wider goals. You'll be in the work yourself: social, email, reporting, website updates, shoots and research. Where deeper expertise is needed, you'll work with our performance specialists. You don't need to be an expert in every area. You do need to know enough to speak confidently about each one, recognise good work and know when to bring in the right person."

  - "Bring ideas and recommendations, rather than simply reacting to client requests."
  - "Help clients understand what their marketing means for the wider business."
  - "Write clear, complete briefs for our design, paid media, SEO, video and development teams."
  - "Plan, write and manage social content across client accounts."
  - "Prepare email campaigns, reports, presentations and client updates."
  - "Make straightforward website and content updates where needed."
  - "Plan photo and video shoots with our in-house content team."
  - "Track work in progress and make sure agreed actions get done."
  - "Review work before it goes to the client. Check it's accurate, on brief and up to standard."
  - "Monitor performance across paid media, SEO, social and web analytics, and explain it in plain language."

  - "Experience working directly with clients or external stakeholders."
